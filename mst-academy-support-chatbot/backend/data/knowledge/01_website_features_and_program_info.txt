MASTERSTROKE ACADEMY — ADDITIONAL SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE
Purpose: New information discovered on the live public website that was not already covered by the supplied support KB.

SOURCE: https://masterstroke.academy/
REVIEW DATE: 2026-08-25

1. PUBLIC PROGRAM POSITIONING
- Masterstroke Academy describes itself as a structured, college-integrated programme.
- The public homepage describes the experience as interactive lessons, live code, rigorous assessments, and a path from cryptography to funded founder.
- Homepage programme metrics: 4 Phases, 21 Modules, 123 SubModules, 130+ Hours.
- The public homepage has a “Fellowship Enrollment” section.
- The homepage advertises “Lifetime access + internship + rewards”.
- Students can choose a track during fellowship enrollment.
- The homepage says students can build a streak and compete on the leaderboard for PPO opportunities.
- The homepage says top performers can receive fellowship bonuses.
- Leaderboard ranking is described as updating automatically from progress in the Learning Tree.

2. PLATFORM FEATURES
- Interactive Learning Tree: visual roadmap from fundamentals to advanced topics.
- Full-screen assessments: immersive/lockdown-mode assessments intended to validate understanding.
- Live Code Execution: students can write and execute Solidity, JavaScript and other code directly in the browser with instant feedback.
- Progress Tracking: completion tracking across all 4 phases and 21 modules with detailed progress analytics.
- Blockchain Focus: the platform is designed around blockchain development, DeFi, NFTs and DAOs.
- Certificate on Completion: the homepage advertises a verifiable certificate after completing the full curriculum.
- 70% pass rule: the next lesson is unlocked only after the required understanding/pass condition is met.
- Live on MST Chain: the homepage says students can deploy and test on an EVM-compatible hybrid Layer-1 built for India.
- College integrated: the structured syllabus is described as aligned with academic and industry standards.
