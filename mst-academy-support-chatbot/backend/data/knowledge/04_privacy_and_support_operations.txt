7. BLOCKCHAIN DATA
Certain information may be permanently recorded on-chain, including:
- Wallet addresses
- Certification records
- Verification records
- Reward distribution records
- Validator participation records

Blockchain records may be publicly visible, independently verifiable, immutable and permanently stored.

8. WEB3 WALLET PRIVACY/SECURITY
Wallet addresses can be associated with Academy activities.
Transactions may be publicly visible on blockchain explorers.
Reward distributions can be traceable on-chain.
Certification records may be linked to wallet addresses.
Users remain responsible for protecting wallet credentials, seed phrases, recovery phrases and private keys.
The Academy states it will never request private keys or recovery phrases.

10. INFORMATION SHARING
The Privacy Policy states that Masterstroke Academy does not sell personal information.
Information may be shared when necessary for legitimate business purposes, including with:
- Hosting/infrastructure providers
- Technical support providers
- Security monitoring providers
- Communication providers
- Payment service providers
- Legal authorities where required by law
- Parties involved in a merger/acquisition/restructuring/asset transfer

The Academy states it does not store complete payment card credentials, banking PINs or similar sensitive payment-authentication information.

11. DATA RETENTION
Information may be retained as reasonably necessary to:
- Provide services
- Maintain educational records
- Verify certifications
- Administer rewards
- Meet legal obligations
- Resolve disputes
- Protect platform security
Some blockchain records may remain permanently available.

12. ACCOUNT DELETION LIMITATIONS
Users may contact the Academy about account-related requests.
However, certification records, reward records and blockchain transactions may remain permanently stored on-chain and generally cannot be deleted.
Information may be removed from Academy internal systems where legally permissible while blockchain records remain unaffected.

13. USER RESPONSIBILITIES
Users are responsible for:
- Account security
- Password protection
- Wallet credential protection
- Accurate information
- Keeping contact information updated
Users must not share passwords, recovery phrases or private keys.
