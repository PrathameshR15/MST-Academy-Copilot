MASTERSTROKE ACADEMY — ADDITIONAL SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE
Purpose: New refund, cancellation, payment-failure and service-access information found on the live Refund Policy page.

SOURCE: https://masterstroke.academy/refund-policy
POLICY EFFECTIVE/UPDATED: January 2025
REVIEW DATE: 2026-08-25

1. NATURE OF SERVICES
The Academy describes its services as digital and may include:
- Digital educational content
- Online learning modules
- Assessments and examinations
- Blockchain-related educational programmes
- Validator learning pathways
- On-chain certifications
- Ecosystem participation opportunities
- Reward-based learning programmes
- Future educational/ecosystem services
- No physical products are shipped.

2. DIGITAL PURCHASE ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
- Purchases are digital in nature.
- Access may be granted immediately after successful payment.
- Educational content becomes available electronically.
- Digital products cannot be physically returned.
- Materials may be accessed/consumed immediately after activation.

3. REFUNDS
- Purchases are generally final.
- Refunds are generally not provided once access to the purchased service has been granted.
- Users are advised to review course descriptions, eligibility requirements and programme details before purchase.
- Non-refundable categories include course purchases, learning tracks, validator programmes, student/professional programmes, certification programmes, premium content access, digital educational resources, downloadable materials, membership plans, reward-based educational programmes and other digital services.

4. REASONS THAT DO NOT BY THEMSELVES CREATE A REFUND
No refund is provided solely because:
- The user changes their mind.
- The user stops learning.
- The user does not complete the course.
- The user fails an assessment.
- The user becomes ineligible for certification.
- The user does not participate actively.
- The user does not use available access.

5. CANCELLATION
- Users may stop using the platform at any time.
- Purchased courses cannot be cancelled after enrollment.
- Access remains active according to the purchased plan.
- No partial or pro-rata refunds are provided.
- Cancellation does not by itself create a refund entitlement.

6. DUPLICATE PAYMENTS
If the same transaction is charged more than once because of a technical/payment-processing issue:
- The user should notify Academy Support promptly.
- After verification, the duplicate transaction may be reversed or the excess amount may be refunded through the original payment method.
- Verification may require transaction references, payment screenshots or other supporting documentation.

7. PAYMENT SUCCESSFUL BUT ACCESS NOT GRANTED
If payment succeeds but the purchased course/programme/service is not activated:
- Contact Academy Support.
- Provide registered email address.
- Provide transaction ID.
- Provide payment screenshot.
- Provide relevant account details.
- After verification, the Academy may activate the purchased service or coordinate with the payment provider.
- The policy says support requests should ideally be submitted within 7 days of the transaction.

8. TECHNICAL/SERVICE INTERRUPTIONS
Temporary interruptions can occur due to:
- Server maintenance
- Network issues
- Payment gateway downtime
- Blockchain congestion
- Wallet integration issues
- Third-party service disruptions
Such interruptions do not automatically qualify for refunds.

9. REWARDS/CERTIFICATION
- Enrollment does not guarantee rewards.
- Enrollment does not automatically guarantee certification.
- Rewards can have eligibility requirements.
- Certification may require successful completion of modules and assessments.
- Validator participation may require additional qualifications.
- Failure to meet eligibility requirements is not by itself grounds for a refund.

10. WALLET-RELATED RESPONSIBILITY
Some services may require a compatible Web3 wallet and valid wallet address.
Users are responsible for wallet accuracy and security. The Academy states it is not responsible for losses caused by incorrect wallet addresses, wallet misconfiguration, loss of credentials/private keys/recovery phrases, unsupported wallets, third-party wallet failures, or blockchain issues outside its control.
Where blockchain assets are distributed, users must provide a compatible wallet. Assets sent to incorrect/incompatible addresses may not be recoverable.
