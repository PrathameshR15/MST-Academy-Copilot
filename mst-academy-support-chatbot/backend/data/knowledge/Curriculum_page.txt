### Detailed Curriculum Breakdown

#### Phase 1: The Internet, Decentralisation & Blockchain Foundation (~18 hrs | 4 Modules, 28 Submodules)

* **Module 1: The Internet Revolution – From Web 1.0 to Web 3.0 (6 lessons)**
1. *The Birth of the Internet: ARPANET to Global Network:* Why it was built decentralized, how it worked, and where centralization crept back in.
2. *Web 1.0 The Read Only Library:* Understanding static content and what blockchain fixes.
3. *Web 2.0 The Social and Centralised Web:* Interactive/social web and the trade-offs in data, freedom, and ownership.
4. *Web 2.0's Hidden Costs:* Platform cuts, free app economics, and reclamation via blockchain.
5. *Web 3.0 - The Ownership Web:* True ownership of data, money, and digital identity.
6. *Web 2 vs Web 3 Showdown:* Practical exercise redesigning WhatsApp as a blockchain dApp.


* **Module 2: Centralisation vs Decentralisation (6 lessons)**
1. *Centralised Systems: YouTube & Google Drive:* Technical mechanics of centralization and single points of failure.
2. *Architecture of Centralisation:* Dissecting central architectural dependencies (e.g., PhonePe-Yes Bank collapse example).
3. *Single Points of Failure & Censorship:* Architectural causes behind system outages (e.g., AWS) and platform bans.
4. *Decentralised Systems: Wikipedia & BitTorrent:* Pre-blockchain decentralized models.
5. *Benefits of Decentralisation:* Cost/speed comparisons (e.g., Western Union ₹1,200 vs. Blockchain ₹8).
6. *Distributed Systems as Blockchain Foundation:* Bridging distributed systems theory and blockchain trust mechanics.


* **Module 3: Blockchain Fundamentals (9 lessons)**
1. *What is a Blockchain?:* From village ration shops to Satoshi's whitepaper.
2. *Blocks, Chains & Transactions:* Block structure, chaining mechanics, and Bitcoin cash vs. Ethereum account models.
3. *Cryptographic Hashing:* SHA-256 and Keccak-256 implementation and inner workings.
4. *Immutability & Tamper-Evidence:* Data persistence mechanisms, promises, and limitations.
5. *Transparency & Pseudo-Anonymity:* Differences between anonymity, pseudo-anonymity, and full transparency.
6. *Consensus Mechanisms:* Deep dive into 8 algorithms (Proof of Work, Proof of Stake, MST's Trika POSA, etc.).
7. *Forks & Network Evolution:* Upgrades, soft/hard forks, and network splits (e.g., ETH/ETC).
8. *Merkle Trees & State Management:* Millisecond verification via Merkle trees and Ethereum single-hash state storage.
9. *Hands-On: Build a Paper Blockchain:* Constructing a 3-block chain by hand, hashing, and browser simulation.


* **Module 4: Cryptography (7 lessons)**
1. *Public Key Cryptography:* Asymmetric keys, wallet mechanics, and Web3 identity.
2. *Digital Signatures:* ECDSA, Ed25519, and Schnorr signature verification.
3. *Hash Functions & Merkle Proofs:* Converting 10,000 pages to a 64-character fingerprint and light client verification.
4. *Zero Knowledge Proofs:* Proving facts without revealing underlying private data.
5. *Commitments & VRFs:* Cryptographic commitments and Verifiable Random Functions for gaming integrity.
6. *Post-Quantum Cryptography:* Mitigating quantum threats to RSA/ECC/ECDSA via NIST standards.
7. *Practical: Build a Wallet CLI:* Lab creating a CLI wallet from scratch (key generation, address derivation, signing, and verification).

* **Module 1: Cryptocurrency & Tokenomics (6 lessons):** Monetary history, Bitcoin, wallet security, token standards (ERC-20, ERC-721, ERC-1155), gas dynamics, and stablecoins.
