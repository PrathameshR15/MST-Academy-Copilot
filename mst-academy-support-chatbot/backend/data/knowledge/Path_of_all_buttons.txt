Here is the master navigation and functional path guide for all major sections, buttons, and features across the Masterstroke Academy platform:

---

### 1. Dashboard & Account Operations

* **Access Profile Settings & Update Personal Info**
* **Path:** `Dashboard` $\rightarrow$ `Sidebar: Profile` $\rightarrow$ `Edit Fields (Name, Phone, Socials, Wallet)` $\rightarrow$ `[Save Changes]`


* **Upload / Update Profile Photo or Resume**
* **Path:** `Dashboard` $\rightarrow$ `Sidebar: Profile` $\rightarrow$ `[Upload Photo]` or `[Choose File]` (under CV section)


* **View Overview Metrics & Daily Progress**
* **Path:** `Dashboard` $\rightarrow$ `Sidebar: Overview` $\rightarrow$ View Streak / Modules / XP Stats


* **Check Live Assessment Submissions & Mentor Grades**
* **Path:** `Dashboard` $\rightarrow$ `Sidebar: Submission Progress` $\rightarrow$ View Live Review Queue


* **Sign Out of Session**
* **Path:** `Dashboard` $\rightarrow$ `Sidebar: Sign Out` $\rightarrow$ Login Screen



---

### 2. Learning Tree & Curriculum Progression

* **Access Main Learning Roadmap**
* **Path:** `Header / Sidebar` $\rightarrow$ `[Start Learning]` or `[Learning Tree]` $\rightarrow$ 4-Phase Architecture


* **Navigate Between Curriculum Phases**
* **Path:** `Learning Tree` $\rightarrow$ Click Phase Card (`Phase 1`, `Phase 2`, `Phase 3`, or `Phase 4`) $\rightarrow$ Phase Modules View


* **Open & View Lessons / Submodules**
* **Path:** `Learning Tree` $\rightarrow$ Select Phase $\rightarrow$ Click Module Card $\rightarrow$ Expanded Submodules Drawer $\rightarrow$ Select Submodule


* **Jump to Active / Latest Uncompleted Lesson**
* **Path:** `Header` $\rightarrow$ `[Start Learning]` $\rightarrow$ Direct Lesson Workspace


* **Reset Roadmap Filter / Return to Course Root**
* **Path:** `Breadcrumb Bar` $\rightarrow$ Click `[Home]` $\rightarrow$ Full Course Overview



---

### 3. Gamification, Community & Leaderboard

* **View Global Rankings & XP Standings**
* **Path:** `Header / Dashboard` $\rightarrow$ `[Leaderboard]` $\rightarrow$ Top Podium & Student Rankings


* **Access Live Events & Webinars**
* **Path:** `Header` $\rightarrow$ `[Events]` $\rightarrow$ Live Sessions & Demo Day Schedule


* **Claim Course Completion Certificate** *(Requires 100% Submodule Progress)*
* **Path:** `Dashboard: Overview` $\rightarrow$ `[Claim Certificate]` $\rightarrow$ On-Chain Certificate Modal



---

### 4. Referral Program & Earnings

* **Generate & Copy Referral Link / Code**
* **Path:** `Dashboard` $\rightarrow$ `Sidebar: Refer & Earn` $\rightarrow$ `[Copy Referral Code]` / `[Copy Link]`


* **Set Self / Referee Discount Allocation**
* **Path:** `Dashboard` $\rightarrow$ `Sidebar: Refer & Earn` $\rightarrow$ Adjust Discount $\%$ $\rightarrow$ `[Update]`


* **Withdraw Earned Referral Commissions**
* **Path:** `Dashboard` $\rightarrow$ `Sidebar: Refer & Earn` $\rightarrow$ `[Request Withdrawal]`



---

### 5. Global Navigation & Site-Wide Functions

* **Return to Public Landing Page**
* **Path:** Top-Left `[Masterstroke Logo]` $\rightarrow$ Academy Main Landing Page


* **Toggle Visual Theme**
* **Path:** Header Control Bar $\rightarrow$ Click `[Sun/Moon Icon]` $\rightarrow$ Toggle Light/Dark Mode


* **Toggle Navigation Drawer (Mobile / Compact View)**
* **Path:** Header Control Bar $\rightarrow$ Click `[≡ Menu Icon]` $\rightarrow$ Expand/Collapse Navigation Drawer


* **Access Legal Policy & Support Pages**
* **Path:** Page Footer $\rightarrow$ Click `[Refund Policy]`, `[Terms & Conditions]`, `[Privacy Policy]`, or `[Contact Us]`
